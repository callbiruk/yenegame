Support & FAQ

Stuck? Most answers are below. If not, email support@yenegame.com and tell us your phone model, app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened.

Purchases

How do I restore Premium on a new phone or after reinstalling?

  1. Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy Premium.
  2. Open YeneCharades and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Restore purchases. Premium unlocks within a few seconds.

Premium Lifetime and active subscriptions can be restored on Android to Android or iPhone to iPhone. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google.

How do I cancel Premium Monthly?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the app. Deleting the app does not cancel.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › YeneCharades › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play › tap your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › YeneCharades › Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play’s order history. See our Terms of Use.

I paid but Premium isn’t showing.

Check your internet connection, then tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn’t unlock, email us your store receipt or order number (starting with GPA. on Google Play) — never send card details.

Playing

Does the game need internet?

No. All cards are inside the app. Internet is only used to download new cards, show ads in the free version and check purchases.

How do I change the card language?

Go to Settings and choose the card language: Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali or English. The app’s menu language can be set separately.

Tilting doesn’t work.

Hold the phone upright against your forehead, screen facing out, and make a clear nod: tilt down for correct, up to pass. The on-screen buttons work too.

A card is wrong, misspelled or offensive.

Thank you for telling us. Email support@yenegame.com with the deck name and the card, and we’ll fix it in the next content update.

Can I suggest new cards?

Yes — use Suggest a card in the app. Our team reviews every suggestion.
