Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Applies to the YeneCharades app for Android and iOS

This policy explains what information the YeneCharades app (“the app”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by YeneGame (“we”, “us”). We designed it to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information we collect

Anonymous install ID

When you first open the app it creates a random identifier on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or store account. We use it to group usage events and purchase status from the same installation. Deleting the app deletes this ID; reinstalling creates a new one.

Gameplay and usage events

Events such as decks and modes played, round length, cards marked correct or passed, screens opened and settings changed. Each batch of events is sent with: the install ID, platform (Android or iOS), app version, app and card language, whether Premium is active, and a push token if you turned on notifications. Our servers may derive an approximate country from your IP address; we do not store precise location.

Card suggestions

If you use “Suggest a card”, we receive the word, the optional deck, language and note you typed, and your install ID, so our moderators can review it. Please don’t put personal information in a suggestion.

Crash and diagnostics data

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier). Firebase Analytics may receive the same gameplay events as a backup.

Advertising data (free version)

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID or, only with your permission, Apple’s IDFA), IP address and ad interaction data to show, limit and measure ads. See Advertising and consent.

Purchase status

Whether you bought Premium, which product, and when it renews or expires. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card number.

Stays on your device

Your settings, team names, scores and on-device leaderboard, custom “My decks”, drawings in Draw it mode and result images stay on your device. Sharing a result uses your phone’s share sheet and only goes where you send it. Pronunciation in Learn mode uses recorded clips or your device’s own text-to-speech voice.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and deliver new cards and decks over the air.
  • To understand which cards are too easy, too hard or confusing, and improve them.
  • To fix crashes and bugs.
  • To show ads in the free version and to unlock Premium when you pay for it.
  • To send the notifications you opted in to.
  • To prevent abuse of our services (for example, blocking an install ID that floods the suggestion queue).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles of you. Where the law (such as the GDPR) requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for personalised ads and notifications, on contract to provide Premium, and on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the game.

3. Advertising and consent

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between games (never in the middle of a round), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Premium removes all ads.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), a certified consent tool, to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalised ads only. You can change your choice from the app’s settings where offered, or by contacting us.
  • United States: where state privacy laws (such as California’s CCPA/CPRA and similar laws in other states) treat personalised advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, Google’s US state privacy messaging may let you opt out. You can also opt out by emailing us. We honour Global Privacy Control signals where the law requires.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt (once, after your first game) before the advertising identifier is used. If you decline, you still see ads, just not personalised ones.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings › Google › Ads.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Purchases

Premium is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat to check your purchase status across app launches and restores; RevenueCat receives the anonymous install ID, the product purchased, transaction identifiers and store receipts, and never your card details.

5. Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings. Weekly “new deck” reminders are scheduled locally on your device. If you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives the app a push token, which we store with your install ID so we can send occasional announcements. Turn notifications off in the app or in your phone’s settings at any time.

7. Children

YeneCharades is a family party game, and it includes a Kids & Family deck. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

  • While the Kids & Family deck is in use, ad requests are tagged as child-directed (consistent with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA), ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and no personalised ads are shown.
  • The app has no chat, no public profiles and no way for children to share personal information with other players.
  • Parents can buy Premium to remove all ads.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, in a card suggestion), email support@yenegame.com and we will delete it.

8. How long we keep data

  • Usage events linked to an install ID: up to 24 months, after which we delete them or keep only aggregated statistics that cannot identify an installation.
  • Card suggestions: until reviewed; accepted words may become part of a deck without any link to you.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Push tokens: until you turn off notifications or the token expires.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide Premium and meet tax and accounting obligations.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app to remove the install ID and everything stored on your device.
  • Ask us to delete data linked to your install ID — see Delete your data.
  • Opt out of personalised ads through the consent options above, or buy Premium for no ads at all.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Email support@yenegame.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for your install ID (shown in the app’s Settings) to find your data.
  • You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to our backend is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the little data we hold.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
