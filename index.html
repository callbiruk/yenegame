About us

Games that sound like home.

YeneGame is a small Ethiopian game studio. We make party games full of Ethiopian culture — the food, music, places, history and everyday jokes that families and friends already share.

YeneCharades · የኔ ቻሬድስ

YeneCharades is our first game: a charades and heads-up style party game. One player holds the phone to their forehead, everyone else acts, describes or hums the word on the card, and the clock is always too short.

Five languages

  • አማርኛ Amharic
  • Afaan Oromoo
  • ትግርኛ Tigrinya
  • Soomaali
  • English

Every card is written in all five, with Latin transliteration.

70+ decks

History, cities, food, faiths, music, daily life, holiday party packs and a Kids & Family deck.

Plays offline

All cards ship inside the app, so a game works at a wedding, in a minibus or anywhere without signal.

Many ways to play

Classic, act it out, describe it with forbidden words, draw it, hum it, and a Learn mode with pronunciation and facts.

YeneCharades is free to play with ads. YeneCharades Premium removes ads and unlocks the Premium decks and modes.

What we care about

  • Respect for culture. Card text is reviewed by native speakers before it is published.
  • Collecting as little as we can. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Read our Privacy Policy.
  • Fair play for families. The Kids & Family deck uses child-directed ad settings.
