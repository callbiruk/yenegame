Privacy

Delete your data

YeneCharades has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. The app uses an anonymous install ID instead. Here’s how to remove the data linked to it.

Step 1 — Find your install ID

Open YeneCharades › Settings and copy the install ID shown under About. If you can’t find it, or you have already deleted the app, you can still write to us: tell us your platform (Android or iOS), the app version and roughly when you used it, and we’ll match what we can.

Step 2 — Email us

Send an email to yenegames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and include your install ID.

Email a deletion request

Step 3 — Delete the app

Uninstalling removes the install ID, settings, scores, custom decks and everything else stored on your phone.

What we delete

  • Usage and gameplay events linked to your install ID.
  • Card suggestions you sent and the install ID attached to them.
  • Your push notification token.
  • Any crash reports and analytics records we can match to your installation in Firebase.
  • Your RevenueCat customer record.

What we may keep

  • Records of purchases that we must keep for tax, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons, for as long as the law requires. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.
  • Aggregated statistics (for example, “this card was passed 40% of the time”) that cannot identify you.
  • Card words we already accepted into a deck, with no link to you.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription or remove Premium Lifetime from your store account. To cancel, see Support.

How long it takes

We confirm your request by email and complete deletion within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire, within a further 30 days.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
