Your data

Delete your YeneLijoch data

YeneLijoch works without an account. In that case everything is stored only on your device: deleting the app deletes it.

If you created a parent account

  1. Open YeneLijoch and go to the parent area (pass the parent gate).
  2. Choose Account → Delete account and all data, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your children's synced profiles and progress, any backed-up family voice recordings, and your consent records. Data is removed from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within a further 30 days. Purchase records that the law requires us to keep are kept as transaction records only.

Can't open the app?

Email yenegames@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject “Delete my YeneLijoch data”. We will confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
