Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to the YeneLijoch app for Android and iOS

YeneLijoch (“the app”) is a learning app for children aged 2 to 12, made by YeneGame (“we”, “us”). We built it to collect as little as possible:

  • No ads, ever. No advertising SDKs, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting. The only usage statistics are anonymous counts that go to our own server, and a parent can switch them off.
  • No chat, no social features. Children cannot contact anyone, and nobody can contact them.
  • Children never create accounts. Only a parent or guardian can create an account, and an account is optional.

1. Who we are

YeneGame. Email: yenegames@gmail.com. We are the operator under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the controller under the EU/UK GDPR.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Information that stays on the device

To play, nothing leaves the device. The app stores the following locally:

  • Child profiles (up to 4): a nickname (it does not need to be a real name), an avatar chosen from our characters (no photos), an age band (2–4, 4–7 or 7–12, never a birth date), and chosen languages.
  • Learning progress: which words, letters and stories a child has seen, items due for review, minutes played, stickers and map progress.
  • Settings: daily time limit, sound, reduced motion and similar choices.
  • Family voice recordings (“grandparent voice”): audio an adult records inside the parent area. The app never records children. The microphone can only be used behind the parent gate.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. Device backups (Google or iCloud backups that you control) may include progress and settings. Voice recordings are excluded from Android cloud backup.

2.2 If a parent creates an account (optional)

A parent account lets you sync progress between devices, keep a subscription on several devices, and back up family voice recordings. When you sign up we collect:

  • Your email address, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us if you use Sign in with Apple or Google. With Apple's “Hide My Email”, we only see a relay address.
  • The date and time you confirmed that you are the child's parent or guardian and accepted this policy.
  • Synced data: the child profiles and learning progress described in 2.1, so they appear on your other devices.
  • Voice backups, only if you turn on backup: the recordings and their labels (which word or story line they belong to), stored in a private storage area that only your account can read.

2.3 Purchases

If you subscribe or buy Lifetime, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card details. Our billing provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase receipt from the store and tells the app which plan is active. To do this it uses a random app user ID, or your account ID if you are signed in. We store the plan, its status and its renewal or expiry date.

2.4 Anonymous usage statistics (parents can switch off)

To improve lessons we count events such as “session completed”, “game completed” and “story finished”, with the game type, age band, language, duration, app version and platform. Each event carries a random install ID that is created on the device, is not linked to an account, and changes if the app is reinstalled. Events never include names, nicknames, voice, free text, device identifiers, the advertising ID or location. Our database does not store IP addresses with these events. Our hosting provider's infrastructure logs IP addresses briefly for security and abuse prevention (see section 5).

A parent can turn statistics off at any time in the parent area under Settings. Under COPPA, this limited information is used only to support the internal operation of the app (16 CFR 312.5(c)(7)), never for advertising or profiling.

2.5 Error reports

If something goes wrong, the app writes an error log on the device. It is sent to us only if a parent chooses to send it from the parent area.

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect children's real names, photos, voices, contacts, precise or approximate location, or advertising identifiers. We do not collect anything for advertising.

4. Children's privacy (COPPA and the UK Children's Code)

  • Children use the app without an account and without giving any personal information.
  • Only a parent or guardian can create an account, link payment, turn on voice backup or change settings, and each of those sits behind a parent gate.
  • Child data that reaches our servers (synced profiles and progress) is provided by the parent through their account, after the parent confirms they are the parent or guardian.
  • Parents can review, change or delete their children's information at any time (section 7).
  • The defaults are high privacy: no account, no backup, statistics limited to anonymous counts.
  • The app has no ads, no in-app links that a child can follow without the parent gate, and no push notifications.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, synced data and voice backups: until you delete them or your account. After deletion, data is removed from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within a further 30 days.
  • Anonymous statistics: up to 13 months, then deleted or aggregated so they no longer relate to an install ID.
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, normally 7 years, kept only as transaction records.
  • Infrastructure logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, normally days to weeks.

7. Your choices and rights

  • In the app (parent area, behind the gate): edit or delete child profiles, turn statistics and voice backup on or off, delete voice recordings, export your data, and delete your account and all associated data.
  • Without the app: email yenegames@gmail.com from the address on the account, or follow https://yenegame.github.io/yenelijoch/delete-data/. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can complain to your data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or your EU supervisory authority.
  • Parents in the US can review their child's information, ask us to delete it, and refuse further collection, under COPPA.

We respond within 30 days.

8. International transfers

Our providers store data in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers from the EU, UK or other regions rely on the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each family can read and write only its own rows, and voice backups sit in private storage that only your account can read. Purchase status can be changed only by our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents in the parent area before the change applies. We will ask for consent again where the law requires.
