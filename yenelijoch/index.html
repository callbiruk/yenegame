Kids learning app

YeneLijoch · የኔ ልጆች

Ethiopian languages, Fidel, history and culture for children aged 2 to 12, through short daily play.

Walia the brave ibex, Gela the funny gelada and Qebero the wise Ethiopian wolf guide Lia and Abel, two siblings growing up abroad, on a journey across Ethiopia. Every region brings new words, songs and stories.

Made for kids

No ads, no tracking, no chat. A parent gate protects purchases, settings and links.

Learn by playing

A 5 to 15 minute daily path: mini-games, a reward, then a story or song, and a gentle goodbye.

Grandparent voice

Family members can record words and stories in their own voice.

Native voices only

Every word is checked by native speakers and recorded by native voice actors. No machine voices.

Languages

  • አማርኛ Amharic
  • ትግርኛ Tigrinya (coming)
  • Afaan Oromoo (coming)
